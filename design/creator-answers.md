Q: Do Clues and Food carry over from one Alone Against Fear run to another, assuming you stay in the same general area and don't travel to a different city?
A: Yes. The game takes place in one city. They do not carry over if a character dies and you start a new one in the same “campaign”.

Q: When you attempt to retreat from an encounter and fail, do the monster(s) then get to attack normally after you took the automatic damage or does the automatic damage count as their turn?
A: The automatic attack is their turn

Q: Can you remain in the same space and encounter the space again without moving out? For example to heal multiple times in a safe house?
A: No each space can be played once unless it says something like "every time you enter this area". In any case for entering you must have exited the area and when you enter an already visited area you must roll for wandering monsters

Q: Do you keep your clues when starting a new mission? Do the number of boxes you’ve moved for food purposes reset to zero when starting a new mission?
A: You keep clues and boxes do not reset. You ate that food or spent those hours without eating. Everything carries on in the campaign

Q: If you avoid an encounter does all the text of the encounter still apply if you revisit the space? For example there is a railway station encounter with zombies that you can choose to ignore but if you kill them you get special loot. If you ignore them and leave would you have the exact same encounter if you came back?
A: If you avoid an encounter the text still applies and the monsters are still there.

Q: Is it possible to have multiple groups of random monsters remain in the same space by having multiple random encounters and escaping them? Would you make more than one stealth roll to avoid them? If you chose not to try stealth, would you fight all of the groups of monsters at once?
A: Yes there could be multiple groups of monsters in the same area and you must roll stealth against each group separately. If you don’t skulk, you fight all of them in waves, one after the other with no rest between waves.
