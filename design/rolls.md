# Rolls and tags

The vocabulary effects use to say which rolls they change. Built as we go: each entry names where it was found. Not exhaustive yet.

## Roll types

- **Attack** — hit a creature, vs its L. Fighter, Good Shot, Bow, Vampire Hunter, Zombie Hunter, Alien Fighter, Impaired arm, Eye damage.
- **Defense** — avoid a creature's attack, vs its L. Agile, Dodge, Alien Fighter, Demon Slayer.
- **Save** — avoid a danger, vs its L. Resilient, Demon Slayer, Will, Agile (climb, jump, avoid danger).
- **Escape** — run away, vs the creature's L. Slippery, Limping, Overconfidence.
- **Stealth** — slip past unnoticed. Stealth.
- **Charisma** — persuade. Charisma, Facial scar.
- **Strength** — lift, break, force. Strong.
- **Magic** — cast a spell or ritual. Occultist, Brain damage.
- **Will** — resist mentally. Will, Brain damage.
- **Search** — Search table roll, not vs L (natural 1 stays 1). Investigator.
- **Surprise** — the creature's x in 6 chance, rolled for the creature. Danger Sense.

## Context tags

- **Weapon**
  - hand-to-hand — Fighter, Impaired arm
  - ranged — Good Shot, Eye damage
  - bow — Bow
  - unarmed — Martial Arts
  - two-handed — Crippled arm
- **Opponent**
  - vampire — Vampire Hunter, Faith
  - zombie — Zombie Hunter
  - alien — Alien Fighter
  - demonic horror — Demon Slayer
- **Danger**
  - poison, infection, disease — Resilient
  - fear — Will, Phobic
  - magic, hypnotism, psionics — Will
  - climb, jump, avoid danger — Agile
  - sanity loss — Phobic

## Fuzzy entries

- Is **Will** a roll type, a kind of Save, or both? The Will Skill says "Will rolls and saves vs Fear, Magic…".
- What counts as **avoid danger** for Agile? Probably any Save the text describes as dodging or falling.
- Will (Expert) drops Hypnotism from its list. Likely a typo.
