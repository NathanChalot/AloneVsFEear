## Decisions

- **Two ID kinds, `skill.` and `arcane_skill.`** (the user): the rules treat them differently, and the ID alone tells which one a character has.
- **The XP cost is a number** (`xp: 8`, the user, against the recommended text field): settles that part of D1 early; the rest stays open until E6.
- **The Expert line is a field of its own** (the user): a note printed after it (Agile, Dodge) moves to the end of `text`, same words.
- **Arcane Skills get `components` and `sacrifice` fields** (the user).
- **Injuries and Mental Scars are named entries with their own IDs** (`injury.limping`, `mental_scar.paranoia`, the user): the character keeps them for life.
- **Literal blocks for several paragraphs or a list** (the user, after asking what it changes): a blank line between paragraphs on screen. R3's texts are aligned in a follow-up PR.
