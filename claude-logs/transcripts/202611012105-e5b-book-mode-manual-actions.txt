⏺ Here's the E5b plan.

  Goal. A player can walk the town in the terminal and apply what each box's
  text says through manual actions that go through the rules, until they die, go
  insane or quit. The manual actions are:
  - gain or lose Life, Sanity, Food, XP or Clues
  - take or drop an item
  - a roll vs L (L and modifier typed in)
  - a Search roll
  - mark the current box as a Safe House

  The turn ends at 0 Life or Sanity.

  Design

  - New module engine/manual.py. One list of manual actions shared by the loop
    and book mode:
    - offered(game) lists only the actions that are allowed right now. You can't
      lose Food you don't have, drop an item when you have none, or mark a box
      that's already a Safe House.
    - apply(game, interface, action) asks what it needs, changes the state
      through Game and tells the interface what happened.
  - Game API additions:
    - roll_vs(level, modifier): a natural 1 fails, a natural 6 succeeds
      (existing ruling), otherwise total ≥ L.
    - search(bonus): d6 + bonus, a natural 1 stays 1, the result is kept between
      1 and 6. It marks the box Searched and returns a Rolled.
    - A SAFE_HOUSE mark.
    - Losses are floored at 0. Losing Food, XP or Clues is capped at what you
      have.
    - Gains are not capped until E6 adds max values.
  - Search is a free action, offered while the box you stand in hasn't been
    Searched. Its entry is then followed like any other entry
    (book_mode.follow(rolled)). Search 1 gets an Auto lead to Random Encounter.
    Search 6 stays in book mode: its tables, plus "gain 1 Clue" by hand. You
    roll the 4 times by picking the table 4 times. E10 writes it properly.
  - Book mode. After an entry the engine can't read, the menu becomes: the
    manual actions, then table, event, "nowhere else". After each manual action
    the menu comes back, so you apply the whole entry before leaving it.
  - Terminal keys. s becomes Search and attaching a sheet moves to n. The manual
    actions get letters, for example g gain, l lose, i take item, o drop item,
    v roll vs L, h Safe House.

  Four questions change what I build. I'll stop for your answers before writing
  any tests.
