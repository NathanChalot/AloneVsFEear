⏺ I recorded two rulings in design/rulings.md (new Effects section) and linked
  them from design/effects.md:
  - Modifiers stack unless the text says otherwise. I searched online and found
    no answer from the author for Alone Against Fear. The search pages were
    about other games, mostly D&D and Pathfinder (There Will Be Games review,
    Four Against Darkness on DriveThruRPG). The search summary mentions a Four
    Against Darkness case that fits: a dwarf's heavy armor gives +2 to Defense
    and he gets another +1 against trolls, ogres and giants. But it came only
    from unofficial copies of the corebook, so it's weak support. The ruling
    rests on the book listing its exceptions one by one.
  - A box of time without moving advances the clock only.

  Reaction order, with examples. A reaction is an effect that acts on its own
  when a moment happens. Sometimes two reactions fire on the same moment and the
  result changes depending on which goes first. Three cases from the book:

  1. Entering a Safe House while infected (death or not). The Sewers infection
     makes you "lose 1 Life every time you cross a box until you reach a Safe
     House". You have 1 Life left and walk into a Safe House. Two reactions fire
     on "enter a box":
     - infection first: you lose 1 Life and die on the doorstep;
     - Safe House first: the infection ends, you heal, and you live.
  2. Combat ends with a grenade thrown and the first aid kit's healing available
     (healed or not before the next fight). The grenade gives a 3 in 6 chance
     of a new encounter after the combat. The first aid kit heals after an
     encounter where you lost Life.
     - Kit first: you heal, then maybe fight again at full strength.
     - Grenade first: you may have to fight the newcomers wounded.
  3. Two independent heals (order doesn't matter). Say you enter a box where a
     mission rule gives Food and Safe House healing also applies. Either order
     gives the same result, so the engine doesn't need to ask.

  The question is what the engine does in cases 1 and 2, where the book is
  silent.

⏺ Here are the three options I gave for a box of time without moving. The book's
  cases are reading an occult book ("uses d3 boxes of time") and performing a
  ritual ("takes 2 boxes of time"), both in 1_3_arcane_skills.md.

  1. Advances the clock only (my recommendation, and what you picked)
     - The box number goes up, so Food every 5 boxes, "for the next N boxes"
       effects and mission deadlines all count it.
     - It is not entering a box: no random encounter roll and no place rules.
     - Deadlines that count only new boxes (the Hell Gate after 12+d6 new boxes)
       ignore it.
  2. Clock plus an encounter roll
     - Everything in option 1, plus a 1 in 6 random encounter for each box of
       time, as when you enter a visited box.
     - Harsher: time spent standing still is dangerous.
  3. Flavour only
     - No effect on the clock. Simplest, but it ignores the book's wording and
       makes reading and rituals free.

  Your pick is recorded as a ruling in design/rulings.md (Effects section), and
  the "Box and time" section of design/effects.md points to it. If you'd rather
  switch to option 2, or mark this one switchable so a horrific campaign mode

  write the session log.
