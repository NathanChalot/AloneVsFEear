⏺ 1. Does the engine handle the character's stats and inventory?

  Nothing is coded yet. In the MVP, though, the engine always holds the whole
  character sheet:
  - Life and Sanity (current and max), Skills, XP, Food, Clues
  - the inventory: hands, item slots, the Food limit, ammo per weapon
  - Injuries and Scars

  Book mode doesn't change that. It only means the engine doesn't know what one
  particular entry does. The numbers stay in the engine either way. In the
  story, the engine counted the Food, the shells and the Life every time, House
  2 included.

  2. Who counts the damage?

  The engine, every time there's a fight. It rolls the Attack, works out hits
  and Damage, takes Life off the creature, rolls your Defense, then runs the
  Damage chain: armor, Luck, the Injury offer, Life. You only make the choices:
  attack or escape, take an Injury or not, how to share Damage in a group.

  That covers book mode too:
  - Creature whose stats are written (all of them in step 1): the engine runs
    the fight; only its special rule shows as text.
  - Creature only in an unwritten text (an event's "d6+3 sewer people, L2, 1
    Life, Damage 1"): the "Fight a creature" action lets you enter those stats,
    and the engine runs the fight.

  The gap: a special rule that acts during the fight. Take the alligator: "every
  attack hitting it has a 2 in 6 chance of bouncing off". The engine can't
  apply it until that rule is written.

  3. Are we adding actions to raise or lower stats?

  Yes, those are book mode's manual actions:
  - gain or lose Life, Sanity, Food, XP, Clues
  - add or remove an item
  - fight a creature
  - mark a Safe House

  They aren't new rules. The engine needs these operations anyway: a written
  result like "heal 1 Life, gain 1 Food" calls exactly the same ones. Book mode
  just lets you call them yourself.
