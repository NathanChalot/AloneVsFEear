  2. Literal or folded block: what changes? Only how the line breaks are stored,
  and so how the terminal shows a text of several paragraphs. The terminal
  prints a text's line breaks as they are and doesn't wrap lines itself.
  - A folded block (>-, R3's way) joins the lines you write into one line. A
    blank line becomes a single line break, so paragraphs show one under the
    other with no gap. A list would also be joined into one line ("- Holy water
    - Incense …"), unless written indented.
  - A literal block (|-, the agent's way) keeps every line break as written.
    Paragraphs show with a blank line between them, and each list item gets its
    own line.
  - The words are the same either way. The only cost is that R3's mission text
    (paragraphs, no gap) and the Skills (paragraphs with a gap) look a little
    different. That's cheap to change later in either direction, since it's only
    formatting.
