## Goal

Moves, numbering, distance, sheets and Food unit tested, built on the E2 state.

## Done

- `engine/town.py`: sheets as 5×6 blocks of one grid, neighbours (8 around, walls where no sheet is attached), distance (larger of the column and row gaps).
- State: `Box` keeps places, Searched and marks; `TownMap` its sheets; `Campaign` Home and the character's position.
- `Game`: `choose_home` (box 1, first sheet, once), `targets(kind)` and `move(to, kind)` (Normal, Escape) returning an `Arrival`, Food upkeep on every 5th box number (1 Food, else 1 Life), `is_full`, `add_sheet`, box memory methods.
- 61 new tests written first (given/when/then); 229 pass, ruff clean. `docs/architecture.md` updated.

## Decisions

- **Food follows the campaign's box number**, so a newcomer shares the schedule of the dead character (the user: boxes are time). Ruling in `design/rulings.md`.
- **A new sheet attaches against any full sheet** (ruling).
- **Distance is the plain grid count**, kept simple: another sheet can still be added later (the user).
- The engine offers the legal targets and the interface chooses; `move` refuses anything else.
