## Decisions

- **Milestone 1 does not block decisions or design** — only the town map matters, and it feeds the map design topic; the character sheet is a design input.
- **Most D-decisions are not worth deciding yet** — tooling, portability and validation become guidelines; content format (plain Python first) and genAI are deferred; saves are settled by the design (save the campaign); map layout and MVP scope become design topics.
- **Design before code** — domain concepts and flows first, in `design/`, then code abstractions.
- **The campaign is the game state** — per the creator answers, map, box counter, Food and Clues carry on across missions; a mission is a goal plus rules layered on top. Missions can be aborted at a Safe House.
- **Two core concepts emerged** — effects (Skills, Injuries, Scars, items and temporary states share five kinds: roll modifier, stat modifier, rule parameter, new action, restriction; plus a lifetime) and game moments (rules reacting to "every 5 boxes", "every time you take Damage"…). Rolls carry a type and context tags so effects can target them.
- **Rulings** (in `design/rulings.md`): hunting works in any box; an Injury or Scar negates the whole loss (switchable, strict mode could cap at 2); Expert costs the Skill's XP again; hand slots are extra; Strong does not raise the Food limit; discarded items may vanish (d6, 1-3); ammo without a weapon is kept; max 3 Safe Houses.
- **Switchable rulings** — taste-based rulings are marked so they can become campaign options (e.g. a horrific mode) later; not designed yet.
- **Simplest design topics first** — character, then inventory; combat next session.
