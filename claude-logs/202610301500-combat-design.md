## Decisions

- **Rulings are tagged** — `interpretation` (the book is unclear), `gap` (the book is silent), plus `switchable` for possible campaign options. Book-stated rules stay in the topic pages, not in rulings.
- **Claude suggests rulings, the user decides** — Claude had written proposals and switchable marks before asking; from now on it asks first.
- **Book rules, not rulings** — one Defense roll per attack of each surviving creature; a natural 1 on Defense always fails, the reroll only checks the fumble; a failed Escape is the creatures' turn (creator answer).
- **Natural 1 fails and natural 6 succeeds on every roll vs L, Attack included** — the book states it under Saves; we checked that no text says otherwise for Attack. A specific text that says otherwise would win.
- **Damage** — the player shares it among members with more than 1 Life; Damage taken goes through armor, Luck, Injury, then Life.
- **One action is the whole turn** — the book uses "1 action" and "1 turn" for the same costs; free actions stay free.
- **Left-behind creatures keep their wounds** (switchable) — the "fully healed" creatures are written as exceptions.
- **Escape** — to a neighbouring visited box, as the book says (any neighbour from the first box). The user first chose "any neighbour, visited or not", then went back to the book. A failed Escape costs one creature's Damage per group failed against.
- **Several groups: you escape every group or none** (switchable) — roll against every group in the box, snuck-past ones included; only the groups failed against deal Damage, the others do not attack that round; from the next round every group fights, the snuck-past ones as a later wave.
- **Waves** — a failed Stealth roll buffs only its group; all waves in a box are one combat for start and end triggers, with XP per group defeated; "current combat" effects end with each wave, the kinder option (in the book only Dodge's -1 is affected).
- **Effects and game moments stay out of scope** — combat lists the moments it hooks into; the general model is a later design topic. The effect kinds hold up, but combat needs effects scoped to one combat or one attack, and effects on creatures.
- **Class diagrams for structures** — flowcharts are for sequences; the creature profile became a class diagram. `character.md` and `inventory.md` could follow.
