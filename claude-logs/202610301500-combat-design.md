## Decisions

- **Effects and game moments stay out of scope** — combat only lists the moments it hooks into; the general model is the next design topics' job.
- **"Round" for a full exchange** — the book uses "turn" for both your action and the exchange.
- **Book rules, not rulings**: one Defense roll per attack of each surviving creature; a natural 1 on Defense always fails, the reroll only checks the fumble; a failed Escape is the creatures' turn.
- **Rulings reviewed with the user**: natural 1 fails and natural 6 succeeds on every roll vs L, Attack included, after checking that no text says otherwise; the player shares Damage among multi-Life members; Damage taken goes armor, Luck, Injury, Life; left-behind creatures keep their wounds (switchable); a failed Stealth roll buffs only its group; waves are separate combats; one action is the whole turn.
- **Escape** — to a neighbouring visited box as the book says (any neighbour from the first box). Several groups is all or nothing (switchable): each failed group deals one creature's Damage, the escaped ones do not attack, and every group is back next round. During waves, an Escape must beat every group in the box, snuck-past ones included; only the groups failed against deal Damage, and if any roll fails the snuck-past groups join the fight as a later wave.
- **The user changed their mind on the Escape destination** — first chose "any neighbour, visited or not", then went back to the book.
- **The effect kinds hold up** — combat adds no new kind, but needs effects scoped to one combat or one attack, and effects on creatures.
