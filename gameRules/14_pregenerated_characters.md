## Sample pregenerated characters

Transcribed from the two filled-in character sheets at the end of the book. They use the [character sheet](12_character_sheet.md), with "Pregenerated Character" as the title and "Mental scars" instead of "Insanities". Empty fields are left out.

### Anna Miller

- **Sanity** 9, **Life** 10
- **Skills**: Will (basic) +1 to Will-based rolls; Occultist (basic) +1 to Magic rolls
- **Weapons**: Pistol, +1; Hammer, +1 vs. zombies and flaming skeletons
- **Ammo**: 6
- **Equipment**: Hammer, 3 stakes, first aid kit with 3 points
- **Food**: 4

### Douglas Harrison

- **Sanity** 8, **Life** 11
- **Skills**: Slippery (+1 to Escape rolls); Strong +2 to Strength rolls, +3 carried items
- **Weapons**: Knife -1; Chainsaw 2 hands. +1 to Attack, Damage 2.
- **Equipment**: Chainsaw: Two handed. +1 to Attack rolls. Damage 2. At the end of any combat, roll a 1 in 6 chance that the noise attracts d6 zombies (L3 Slow Undead). Bag of salt (6 doses). Knife (-1 to Attack rolls).
- **Food**: 4
- **Books read**: Computer printout of "They Are Among Us".

### Differences with the rules

Transcribed as printed. Where they disagree with the rules, the rules files are the reference:

- Douglas's Strong reads "+2 to Strength rolls, +3 carried items". The [skills](1_2_skills.md) give Strong +1 and +3, Strong (Expert) +2 and +5.
- Anna has no knife, though every character starts with "a knife or club/stick in addition to the weapon rolled" ([weapons](1_4_weapons.md)). Her hammer may stand in for the club.
- Anna's hammer appears both under Weapons and under Equipment.
- Their starting items do not follow the 2 random items of character creation: Anna has three Random Objects (hammer, stakes, first aid kit), Douglas has salt and a book already read.
