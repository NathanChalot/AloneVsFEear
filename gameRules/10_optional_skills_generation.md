## Optional Random Skill Generation

If you prefer, instead of picking Skills, you can generate your character's initial Skills randomly. Roll d6 for each Skill. On a 1-2, roll on table 1; on a  3-4,  roll  on  table  2;  and  on  a  5-6,  roll  on  table  3.  Then  roll  on  the appropriate table. All Skills are at Basic level.

| Random initial skill table 1 (d6)   | Random initial skill table 2 (d6)      |
|-------------------------------------|----------------------------------------|
| 1 Agile                             | 1 Fighter                              |
| 2 Bow                               | 2 Good Shot                            |
| 3 Charisma                          | 3 Investigator                         |
| 4 Danger sense                      | 4 Medic 5 Occultist                    |
| 5 Dodge or Will (choose)            | 6 Resilient or Vampire Hunter (choose) |
| 6 Faith or Will (choose)            | 6 Resilient or Vampire Hunter (choose) |

## Random initial skill table 3 (d6)

- 1 Resistant to Hunger
- 2 Strong Grip on Reality
- 3 Slippery
- 4 Stealth
- 5 Strong
- 6 Tough or Zombie Hunter (choose)
