## Useful stuff table (d66)

- 11 d6 jars of cat food (all jars count as 1 carried item). They may be used as 1 Food each or to avoid one encounter with a cat-like monster.
- 12 Flower bunch/present . Use once to gain a +1 on a Charisma roll.
- 13 Small bell . Useful for some spells.  Negligible weight.
- 14 Backpack . You may carry 15 objects instead of 10.
- Body armor . The body armor has 4 Life points. Every time you take Damage, roll d6. On a 1-3, you take Damage normally. On a 4 or 5, you
- 15 take the Damage off the body armor. On a 6, the Damage bounces off the body armor and is ignored. When the body armor loses its last Life, it is rendered useless. Wearing body armor counts as carrying 2 objects.
- 16 Butterfly collection .
- 21 Martial arts handbook. Reduce by 1 XP the cost for learning the Martial Arts Skill. No reduction on Martial Arts Skill (Expert).
- Antique flail weapon . If you roll a 6, it inflicts 2 Damage instead of 1. If you roll a 1 when attacking, however, you hit yourself for 1
- 22 Damage. You may spend 2 XP to train with the flail and remove the chance of hitting yourself. You may spend an additional 2 XP to become a Flail (Expert) and inflict 2 Damage on an Attack roll of 5 or 6.
- 23 Cheap holy symbol/beads. You may use it against creatures that are susceptible to holy symbols, but if you roll a 1 it breaks and must be discarded.  Negligible weight.
- 24 Toy. You may sacrifice it to gain a +1 on an Escape roll or Charisma roll with any encounter described as a 'child' or 'kid'.
- 25 Box of essential oils. Useful for some rituals.
- 26 Book of Inspirational Quotes . If you spend 1 XP and read this, you gain +1 to your next 3 rolls to avoid Sanity loss. After you have used this bonus 3 times, even if the rolls fail, the bonus is lost and you do not again any other benefits from reading this book.
- 31 Egyptian scarab. You gain +1 to Defense rolls against mummies. After an encounter with a mummy, the scarab loses its powers and the bonus is lost. Negligible weight.
- 32 Dog toy . You may sacrifice it to gain a +1 on an Escape roll to flee from an encounter with any dog or hound creature.
- 33 Stuffed bird . If you meet a bird-shaped demon, its first attack will be directed at the Stuffed bird, which will be destroyed.
- Helmet .  This full biker's helmet has 3 Life points and can protect your head. Every time you take Damage, roll d6. On a 1-5, you take Damage normally. On a 6, the helmet takes the Damage instead. When the
- 34 helmet loses its last Life, it is rendered useless. If worn, the weight of the helmet is negligible. If carried, it counts as 1 object. If the helmet and the body armor are worn in conjunction, on a 3, 4, or 5 you take Damage off the body armor; on a 6 you take Damage off the helmet.
- 35 d3 bottles of liquor . If you drink a whole bottle before an encounter, you will fight at -1 on Attack and Defense rolls, but you may ignore the first point of Sanity lost in that encounter. While drunk, you may not perform Exorcism or any ritual, and you may not learn anything from books. The effect lasts for d6 boxes. You may shorten its duration by 1 box by drinking coffee.
- 36 Strong  coffee .  Drink  to  reduce  the  effect  of  liquor,  above.  This  is enough for drinking 6 times.
- 41 2d6 candles . Useful for many spells.
- 42 Flashlight . This is a source of light; it may be used as a ranged weapon to attack  shadow  creatures  at  +2,  Damage  1.  After  each  encounter  with shadow creatures, there is a 1 in 6 chance of the batteries running out.
- 43 Box of matches. Useful to start a fire or to use a Molotov cocktail.
- 44 Grooming/beauty products as appropriate to your gender. When you use this, you get a +1 to your single next Charisma roll. 
- 45 Bag of marbles. You may sacrifice them to gain a +1 to an Escape roll against any creature that walks. Does not work with slithering, crawling, flying or immaterial creatures (ghosts, giant bugs, tentacled horrors, giant slugs, swarms).
- 46 Bandages, plasters, disinfectant. Sacrifice them to heal 1 Life.
- 51 Protein bars. This count as 1 Food point, negligible weight.
- 52 d6 Energy drinks . These count as 1 Food point each. However, they should be alternated with regular Food points. If you use them twice in a row, you get a -1 on Magic rolls until the end of the mission.
- 53 Kitchen knife. Damage 1, -1 on Attack rolls. It breaks if you roll a 1 on the Attack roll.
- 54 Cell phone. There is no reception or electricity to recharge it. Negligible weight.
- d6 painkillers. For each painkiller taken, roll d6. If you roll any 1, you gain -1 on all your die rolls for the next 3 boxes. If you roll 2-4, no effect. On a 5 or 6, heal 1 Life for each 5 or 6 rolled. If you take
- 55 additional doses and roll any 1 while under the effect of painkillers, not only you get the -1, you also lose 2 Life. You are considered under the effect of painkillers as long as you have a -1 to die rolls because of painkillers.
- 56 Dark clothes. Once per game, you can claim a +1 bonus to a Stealth roll if you wear these clothes. You may wear only one set of clothes at a time. Worn clothes have negligible weight. Carried clothes count as 1 carried object.
- 61 Stylish  clothes. Once  per  game,  you  can  claim  a  +1  bonus  to  a Charisma roll if you wear these clothes. You may wear only one set of clothes at a time. Worn clothes have negligible weight. Carried clothes count as 1 carried object.
- 62 Sport clothes. Once per game, you may claim a +1 bonus to an Escape roll if you wear these clothes. You may wear only one set of clothes at a time. Worn clothes have negligible weight. Carried clothes count as 1 carried object.
- 63 Hockey stick/ golf club . This may be used as a two-handed weapon. Damage 1, or Damage 2 on an explosive die roll (6 on the Attack roll).
- Molotov cocktail . This can be thrown as a one-use weapon. It allows you to automatically destroy 1 zombie or scarecrow or perform an
- 64 Attack roll at +2 against any other opponent. You need a lighter or a box of matches to use this. This is considered fire Damage, so it can destroy vampires permanently.
- 65 Silver ring . May be turned into a single silver bullet for a pistol or rifle if you have smelting equipment or just used in a shotgun shell. Negligible weight.
- 66 Gas Mask . If you wear it, it does not count as a carried item, and you are immune to the effect of any gas or respiratory agent. If you carry it, it counts as a carried object and it can be worn in 1 Action.
