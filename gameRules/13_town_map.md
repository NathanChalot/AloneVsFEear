## Town map

The sheet is a grid of **30 boxes: 5 columns and 6 rows**, filling a portrait page. All boxes are the same size, and the grid has no labels, coordinates or pre-printed content.

- Each box is a square with a rounded top-left corner (like a page curl). The box is blank and meant for notes.
- Each box has a **small white circle at the bottom right**, where the box number is written ("Every time you move in a box, number it… in the small white circle at the bottom right of the box", [how the game works](5_how_the_game_works.md)).
- The background is a faded aerial photo of a town, running across the whole grid. Streets and blocks continue from one box to the next: the boxes cut one continuous town, not separate places. It is decoration only, with no game effect.
- Boxes touch their neighbours on all sides, with no gaps or paths drawn between them. Nothing on the sheet marks edges, exits, roads or special boxes.
