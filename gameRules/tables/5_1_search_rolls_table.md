## search rolls table (d6)

| d6 | Result |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Roll on the random encounter table. |
| 2-5 | Nothing found. |
| 6 | Success! Roll 4 times on the Useful Items table or on the Random Object table, and pick a single result, or Roll on the Random Book table if you are in a Library; OR gain 1 Clue. |

Roll at +1 if you have the Investigator Skill, +2 if you have Investigator (Expert). A roll of 1 remains a 1.
