## Alone against fear Optional rules

Slashers optional rule : Every time a Slasher kills your character, the Slasher gains +1 to either their L or Life points (roll d6, 1-3 apply the bonus to L, 4-6 apply it to Life). However, your next character gets -1 to the original Clue cost for hunting down that Slasher. So for example, a L4 Slasher becomes L5 but still counts as L3 for purposes of calculating his/her Clue cost (so you can find him/her with 3 Clues). Maybe your new character is a relative/friend of the previous one who is hunting down the Slasher in a quest for revenge.

98 Player  character  death  optional  rule :  Every  time  one  of  your characters is killed by a zombie, werewolf, or vampire, s/he will become a monster of the same type and reside in the area where s/he was killed. Your  next  character  will  encounter  the  monstrous  version  of  the character  in  that  area  (after  resolving  any  random  encounter).  The character will have L equal to the L of the monster who killed him/her, Life equal to the maximum Life s/he had when s/he was alive, and other abilities as per the monster type. Destroying a former character gives you 1 XP. Loot will be all the equipment possessed by the character at the moment of death, minus any Food.
